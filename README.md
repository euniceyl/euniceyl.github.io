# Eunice Lee

**Computational scientist** · Associate in Biomedical Informatics @ Harvard Medical School

**Website:** [euniceyl.github.io](https://euniceyl.github.io/)

Hi! I'm a computational scientist transforming multimodal omics data into clinically meaningful insights. My research aims to **advance precision oncology** by developing frameworks at the intersection of **statistical machine learning, spatial biology, and interactive visualization.**

As an associate in the [HIDIVE Lab](https://hidivelab.org/), Harvard Medical School Department of Biomedical Informatics, my current work focuses on creating scalable, open-source software that redefine cell-cell interactions in multiplexed 3D microscopy through surface-based modeling of cellular neighborhoods and interaction spaces. I graduated from Duke University with a Bachelor of Science in Statistical Science (Data Science concentration), and minors in Chemistry and Computational Biology & Bioinformatics.

Alongside my research, I am a [music producer](https://linktr.ee/prodbyeunice) and multi-instrumentalist with a special passion for musical theater. This pursuit stems from my belief that music, much like medicine, has the power to heal. I am currently writing my next original musical while launching live performance projects based in Boston and NYC. Always open to chat or collaborate!
